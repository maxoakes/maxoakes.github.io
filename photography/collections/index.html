My images are stored on my self-host server in a Noodle Gallery Docker container. When this page is loaded, a Javascript script fetches all my shared albums via an API call. If my server is reachable (i.e. turned off), everything that I have shared will be shown below. Therefore, you can come to this page for my most recent adventure updates!

My photo collections are themed by trip taken, and region taken. As such, if you look though each album, there will be repeat images.

  • The "All Day Trips" album and the year-dated albums will show all my images grouped by when they were taken.
  • The "Region" albums group the images by where they were taken.

Navigating the photo albums is very straight forward:

  • Click on a polaroid below to open a photo collection.
  • Once on the album website, click an image to maximize it and use the left and right arrow keyboard keys to scroll through the photos. You can press F11 (or whatever your browser default may be) to go fullscreen for a better viewing experience.
  • You can also click the map icon on the upper right of the page to see the geolocation of each photo in the album.
