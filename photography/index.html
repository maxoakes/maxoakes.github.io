Travel Photography

I started exploring the Pacific Northwest in Spring 2024, county-by-county, in an effort to explore all of Oregon. Very soon after I started, I invested in my first camera: a Sony ɑ6100 with the kit lenses. After exploring some of the Willamette Valley and one trip to central Oregon, I got a telephoto lens.

Below are two ways to see my photo album collections.

  • The first link (left) shows all of my shared albums in a list. Single-click a polaroid to open the album.
  • The second link (right) shows all of my location-based photo albums. All of the albums on the globe are listed on the left-side menu. Click one to zoom to its location on the globe. Then, to open the photo album, double-click the polaroid on the globe.
    • The little marker pieces on the globe represent the cities or communities I have visited (green-colored), or those I have yet to visit (red-colored).

Please note that all images are stored on on my self-hosted Docker server. If there is an error message, my server is not accessible at the moment!
